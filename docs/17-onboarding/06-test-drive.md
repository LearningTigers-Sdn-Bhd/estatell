# Test drive (removed)

Test drive was removed from onboarding on 2026-10-16. It was planned as a practice mode on the real pages before go-live, with a practice business date, coach marks and `ClearPracticeData`.

Owners and partners' prospects try the product on the shared [demo properties](../01-platform/06-demo-properties.md) instead. The go-live approval setting is the check before go-live (see [Go live](03-go-live.md#approval)).

The wizard now ends at section 13, Review. See [Wizard steps](02-wizard-steps.md#phases-and-sections).
